Fixed hardware profile
======================

Pluto TX2A          -> CN0566 TX_IN
CN0566 TX_OUT_2     -> external Vivaldi X-band antenna
CN0566 RX1          -> Pluto RX1A
CN0566 RX2          -> Pluto RX2A

Maximum-throughput default:
    Pluto complex TX channel [1]  = physical TX2A
    Pluto complex RX channel [1]  = physical RX2A
    CN0566 TX switch GPIO 0       = TX_OUT_2

Experimental MRC mode:
    Pluto complex RX channels [0,1] = RX1A and RX2A

RF frequency plan:
    Pluto TX/RX LO: 2.200000 GHz
    CN0566 LO:      12.400000 GHz
    OFDM DC carrier is nulled
    Nominal X-band center: 10.200000 GHz
